menu_book Rooted in Scripture • Shining Since 1974

A Gospel Beacon in Kitale, Radiating Faith & Overflowing in Grace

The Africa Inland Church (AIC) Nuru Kitale carries a sacred heritage of evangelistic zeal, prayerful endurance, and steadfast commitment to biblical truth. Named “Nuru” — the Swahili word for Divine Light — our church family has grown into a radiant sanctuary of over 850 believers shining Christ's love across Trans-Nzoia County and Western Kenya.

We exist to magnify Jesus Christ through sound expository teaching, passionate worship, intentional family discipleship, and transformative community compassion across Kitale and beyond.
