## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a dignified, Christ-centered digital presence that bridges timeless reverent faith with contemporary communal warmth. Crafted for Africa Inland Church Ngoro Gitale, the visual ethos avoids gaudy revivalist clichés in favor of architectural composure, editorial grace, and accessible warmth.

The aesthetic philosophy fuses **Modern Editorial Warmth** with subtle **Glassmorphism**:
- **Dignified Serenity:** Deep royal blues evoke stability, steadfastness, and enduring faith.
- **Divine Hope:** Warm burnished gold highlights provide celestial warmth, accentuating celebration, tithes, and sacred promises without overwhelming usability.
- **Communal Grounding:** Verdant emerald touches represent growth, life, ministries, and fellowship.
- **Modern Fellowship:** High tactile polish—featuring frosted top bars, soft card radiuses, and ambient drop shadows—delivers an experience that feels as inviting on mobile devices as it is majestic on wide desktop displays.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional balance between solemn reverence and radiant hospitality.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#0F2942`) — Royal Deep Blue:** Dominates structural anchors, primary buttons, major headings, and primary brand accents.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37`) — Burnished Gold:** Used selectively for high-honor callouts, active scripture highlights, giving CTA accents, and badge outlines. Never use for small body copy.
- **Tertiary (`#2E7D32`) — Muted Emerald:** Designates life, community events, ministry affiliations, growth tracks, and localized badge metadata (e.g., M-PESA confirmations, active registrations).
- **Neutral Dark (`#1A202C`) — Charcoal Stone:** The primary body text color on light backgrounds, preventing the stark harshness of pure black while guaranteeing WCAG AAA legibility.
- **Surface Light (`#F8F9FA` & `#FFFFFF`):** Warm crisp alabaster and pure white create breathable, light-filled sanctuary surfaces.
- **Surface Dark (`#0A1926`) — Deep Midnight Navy:** Reserved for immersive hero headers, evening devotionals, responsive mobile drawers, and anchored footer structures.

## Typography

The typographic pairing provides deliberate contrast:
- **Playfair Display:** Carries scriptural gravitas, pastoral authority, sermon themes, and sacred literature. Its gentle serifs reflect the tradition of printed Bibles and hymnals.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Provides welcoming modern legibility for schedules, ministry listings, M-PESA payment flows, announcements, and UI utilities.
- Italic styling of `Playfair Display` is exclusively reserved for Holy Scripture verses, benedictions, and devotional quotations.

## Layout & Spacing

A responsive 12-column grid governs tablet and desktop viewports, downscaling to a 4-column structure on mobile devices:
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Max container width `1280px`, centered with `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Fluid grid with `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with edge `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.

Vertical pacing embraces airy, reverent breathing room. Section breaks employ generous padding (`space-xl` scaled dynamically) to prevent visual crowding and give sacred text room to resonate.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines ethereal translucent glass with warm, natural drop shadows:

- **Surface Translucency (Frosted Glass):** 
  Navigation headers and sticky service action bars use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a `1px` border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)` on light layouts, or `rgba(10, 25, 38, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)` over dark sections.
- **Ambient Sacred Shadows:**
  - **Level 1 (Cards, Badges):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.06), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`
  - **Level 2 (Floating Action Cards, Giving Modules):** `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.10), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.05)`
  - **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(10, 25, 38, 0.22)`

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, hospitable curvature (level 2):
- Standard cards, pastoral profiles, and media containers utilize `14px` - `16px` border radii (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`).
- Micro-elements such as calendar event chips, filter tabs, and ministry category tags utilize pill shapes (`rounded-full`) to express welcoming community interaction.
- Form controls and inputs match the card aesthetic at `10px` to `12px` border radius.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Deep Royal Blue background (`#0F2942`), pure white text, subtle gold border on hover (`#D4AF37`), border radius `12px`.
- **Secondary / Giving CTA:** Burnished Gold background (`#D4AF37`), Royal Deep Blue text (`#0F2942`), font weight 600.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in Royal Blue or Gold, providing clean structural balance for secondary options.

### Giving & M-PESA Integration Cards
- Dedicated giving modules feature an elevated white card surface with a subtle gold perimeter hairline (`#D4AF37` at 20% opacity).
- Pre-filled denomination chips use pill styling.
- The M-PESA paybill container emphasizes security and clarity, featuring bold monospaced account tokens and emerald status checkmarks for verified transactions.

### Sermon & Media Cards
- Featured media cards provide an 16:9 thumbnail preview with a frosted glass floating duration chip at the bottom right.
- Include integrated Play icon badges with a subtle gold hover ring, pastor speaker metadata, scripture tag chips, and instant downloadable sermon note actions.

### Pastoral Leadership Cards
- Vertical cards showcasing warm, dignified portraits with soft rounded tops (`16px`).
- Include a delicate hairline divider in `#D4AF37` separating the pastoral title from office hours and contact actions.

### Event Schedule & Calendar Chips
- Dates formatted in compact stack badges: top block displaying month in Muted Emerald (`#2E7D32`), bottom displaying numeric day in bold Deep Blue.
- Event list items implement subtle hover elevation, clear location stamps (e.g., "Main Sanctuary", "Youth Hall"), and one-click "Add to Calendar" triggers.
