forum From the Pastoral Desk

“Karibu Sana! No One Was Meant to Walk This Journey Alone.”

“You are the light of the world. A town built on a hill cannot be hidden.”

— Matthew 5:14

Whether you are stepping into a church for the first time in years, moving your family to Trans-Nzoia County, or seeking a loving sanctuary to anchor your spiritual walk, we welcome you with open arms and joyful hearts.

At Africa Inland Church Nuru Kitale, we are bound together not by perfection, but by the transformative grace of Jesus Christ. Our mandate is simple yet eternal: to build passionate disciples, honor families, and shine God’s light in the marketplace, schools, and homes of our beloved town.

When you join us this Sunday, expect heartfelt African praise, reverent scriptural preaching, and a warm cup of Kenyan chai at our visitor lounge. We cannot wait to meet you personally.

In His Radiant Grace,

Rev. Samuel & Mama Grace Adebayo

Living as Children of Light: Walking Wisely in a Dark World

SA

Rev. Samuel Adebayo

Senior Pastor • English & Swahili Summary

In this practical sermon, Pastor Samuel unfolds the call of the believer to reject hidden deeds of darkness and instead live with integrity, fruitful love, and radiant spiritual courage in workplace and family life.

Key Scripture Points
  • 1. From spiritual darkness to Christ’s radiant dawn
  • 2. The fruit of the Light: goodness, righteousness, and truth
  • 3. Awakening from spiritual slumber in daily life
