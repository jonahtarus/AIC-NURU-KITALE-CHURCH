Stewardship Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about giving records, in-kind agricultural gifts, and confidentiality.

At the close of each calendar year, our Church Finance Office prepares certified individual giving receipts upon request. Simply ensure you consistently use your registered name or phone number on M-Pesa and bank transactions. You can also write directly to treasurer@aicnuru.org with your transaction dates.
Yes, absolutely! Kitale is an agricultural community, and our deacons receive dry grains, bagged maize, fresh harvest bundles, and produce at the church office warehouse every Sunday morning before 10:00 AM. These gifts are either distributed to the church benevolence food basket or auctioned with all proceeds credited to your desired designated fund.
If an account designation is left blank, your contribution is automatically allocated to the General Church Sunday Offering fund to support church maintenance and pastoral ministries. If you intended it for the Building Expansion or a specific pledge, forward your Safaricom M-Pesa confirmation message to our Treasury Hotline (+254 712 345 678) within 48 hours for immediate journal reallocation.
All contributions are managed with strict confidentiality in adherence to Kenya's Data Protection Act and biblical stewardship principles. Only the church treasurer and Senior Pastor have access to individual pledge accounts. No personal donation figures are disclosed publicly.
