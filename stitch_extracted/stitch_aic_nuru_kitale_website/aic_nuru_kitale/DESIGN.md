## Brand & Style

This design system reflects the identity of a vibrant, reverent, and community-rooted Christian congregation in Kitale, Kenya. Translating *Nuru* ("Light" in Swahili), the design pairs classical spiritual gravitas with radiant, welcoming warmth. The experience balances liturgical dignity and modern African community fellowship, ensuring digital worship resources, sermon archives, giving gateways, and community groups feel accessible, sacred, and grounded.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Editorial Warmth** with **Gentle Tactile Radiance**:
- **Radiant Dignity:** High-contrast, stately navy paired with luminous warm amber accents evokes dawn breaking over Mount Elgon, bringing physical and metaphorical light to the interface.
- **Approachable Sanctuary:** Generous whitespace, ivory backgrounds, and warm, unhurried typography eliminate digital clutter, fostering contemplation and trust.
- **Regional Grounding:** Designed natively for the Kenyan context, incorporating seamless flows for M-Pesa givings, weekly fellowship fellowships (jumuiyas), and low-bandwidth resilience without sacrificing elegance.

## Colors

The palette establishes an atmosphere of reverent sanctuary and luminous clarity.

- **Primary (`#122B44` - Deep Royal Navy):** The anchor of authority, reverence, and depth. Used for major app headers, primary structural containers, footers, and prominent text elements.
- **Secondary (`#C59331` - Radiant Nuru Gold):** The manifestation of light. Used purposefully on high-value calls-to-action (Giving, Live Stream, Event Registration), interactive highlights, badges, and active tab indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#2B8C67` - M-Pesa & Growth Verdant):** A localized ceremonial green providing functional recognition for M-Pesa giving interactions, confirmation states, and community outreach tags.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1A2B3C` - Deep Rich Slate):** High-legibility text tone replacing harsh black with a soft, deep charcoal navy.
- **Neutral Muted (`#5A6B7C` - Slate Charcoal):** Secondary typography, metadata, timestamps, and subtle borders.
- **Canvas & Surface:**
  - Base background canvas: Soft Alabaster (`#FAF8F5`).
  - Elevated surfaces & cards: Pure Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Subtle warm tint highlight: Luminous Cream (`#FDFBF7`).

## Typography

The pairing creates an immediate dialogue between classic liturgical reverence and welcoming contemporary fellowship:

- **Playfair Display (Headlines & Scriptural Quotes):** Selected for its graceful serif contrast, warm transitions, and noble presence. Used exclusively for sermon titles, hero titles, verse pull-quotes, and section narratives.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (UI, Body, and Functional Labels):** Soft, open geometric curves provide high legibility across varied screen sizes and low-bandwidth mobile views, maintaining warmth and approachable clarity.
- **Typographic Scale:** Line heights prioritize breathing room (`1.5` to `1.6` on body copy) to allow meditative reading of weekly bulletins and devotional scriptures without optical fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy implements a **Fluid Adaptive Grid** structured around uncluttered breathing room and predictable content discovery:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with `2rem` (32px) margins and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Maximum content canvas spans 1240px to retain optimal reading lengths for articles and devotional texts.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Two-column card arrangements for sermon archives and upcoming ministry events.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` outer canvas margin and `1rem` gutters. Stacked single-column hierarchy optimized for thumb-reach navigation during mobile live-streaming and fast M-Pesa transactions.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Generous vertical section padding (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) isolates discrete spiritual and operational topics, preventing sensory overload.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Soft Warm Ambient Shadows** layered across warm tonal surfaces, avoiding cold drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - `Base`: `#FAF8F5` canvas for the foundation.
  - `Surface 1 (Cards & Modules)`: `#FFFFFF` with a faint warm halo outline: `1px solid rgba(18, 43, 68, 0.06)`.
  - `Surface 2 (Floating Modals & Dropdowns)`: Crisp `#FFFFFF` elevated with deep ambient diffusion.
- **Shadow Tokens:**
  - *Subtle Card Rest:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(18, 43, 68, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(18, 43, 68, 0.04)`.
  - *Card Hover & Focused Action:* `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(18, 43, 68, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(197, 163, 49, 0.08)`.
  - *Modal & Drawer:* `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(18, 43, 68, 0.16)`.
- **Luminous Radiance Layer:** Hero sections, giving confirmation banners, and live-broadcast cues may utilize an ultra-subtle radial gradient glow: `radial-gradient(circle at top, rgba(197, 163, 49, 0.12), transparent 70%)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Warm Organic Geometry** that feels soft and welcoming without becoming juvenile:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, search fields, segmented pickers, and small inline badges.
- **Container Radius (1rem / 16px - `rounded-lg`):** Standard for sermon video cards, event cards, giving selection tiles, and dropdown menus.
- **Hero & Modal Radius (1.5rem / 24px - `rounded-xl`):** Dedicated to prominent cards, hero banners, and devotional spotlight containers.
- **Pill (Full Radius):** Reserved for category tags, sermon series chips, live indicators, and localized payment service badges (e.g., M-Pesa status).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Radiant Gold):** Solid background of `#C59331` with `#122B44` bold text. On hover, smooth transition to `#D4A345` with subtle golden glow shadow. Height: 48px desktop / 44px mobile.
- **Secondary (Royal Navy):** Background of `#122B44` with `#FAF8F5` text. Used for secondary liturgical actions (e.g., "Listen to Sermon", "Download Bulletin").
- **Outline / Ghost:** 1.5px border of `#122B44` with transparent background and `#122B44` text.
- **M-Pesa Action Button:** Tertiary green accent (`#2B8C67`) with white text and M-Pesa icon mark, specifically reserved for Tithes, Offerings, and Building Fund contributions.

### Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` background, `rounded-xl` (16px), bordered with `1px solid rgba(18, 43, 68, 0.06)`. Internal padding uses `space-lg` (24px).
- **Sermon Card:** 16:9 thumbnail top container, sermon series pill badge overlaid in top-left, title in Playfair Display (`title-lg`), speaker and scripture references in muted charcoal (`body-sm`), followed by audio/video play action.

### Badges & Chips
- **Sermon Topic & Ministry Chips:** Rounded-full, background `rgba(18, 43, 68, 0.05)`, text `#122B44`, transitioning on selection to solid `#122B44` with white text.
- **Live Now Badge:** Radiant pill with amber glow pulse, text in bold `#C59331`.
- **M-Pesa Express Badge:** Rounded-full pill, light mint background (`#EAF5F0`), forest green text (`#2B8C67`), and localized Paybill indicator.

### Input Fields & Giving Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Crisp white background with `1px solid rgba(90, 107, 124, 0.25)`, `rounded-md` (8px). On focus: border shifts to `#C59331` with a `2px` faint outer ring of `rgba(197, 163, 49, 0.2)`. Label set in `label-sm` slate navy.
- **Preset Giving Amount Selectors:** Grid of rounded square tiles with denomination presets (e.g., KES 200, KES 500, KES 1,000, KES 5,000). Selected state uses deep navy border with gold checkmark indicator.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Form Controls:** 20px size with 6px border-radius for checkboxes, circular for radios. Inactive border is `rgba(90, 107, 124, 0.4)`. Active fill uses `#122B44` with a sharp gold or pure white glyph check.

### Scripture Pull-Quotes
- **Callout Block:** Left border 4px in Radiant Gold (`#C59331`), background tint in warm ivory (`#FDFBF7`), text in italicized Playfair Display with book reference in Plus Jakarta Sans bold uppercase label.
